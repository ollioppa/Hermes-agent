# Documentation Guide — method

**Readable for humans.** Apply this instruction-based method in the writing step you already use. No special assistant, installation, renderer or additional agent is required. Maintained method files are English; documents use the readers' agreed language. [REFERENCE.md](REFERENCE.md) explains the preservation decisions for maintenance, not mandatory reading for every edit. Start with [README.md](README.md).

## Establish the assignment

Read the request, authoritative subject sources/design, current document and prior decisions. Establish readers, primary starting state, outcome, language and destination; reuse known answers. Extract purpose, mechanisms, alternatives and limits before choosing an example. Distinguish intended design, implemented behavior and observed results. Do not invent superiority, uniqueness or measured savings.

For an adoption guide, explain the specific contribution compared with the familiar alternative, including when that alternative is sufficient. The reason must stand without the example. An example cannot redefine the subject's primary scope. For a how-to, establish the task and result; an adoption pitch is unnecessary.

If a material gap remains, use the assignment's available research tools within their permissions or ask focused questions. An optional discovery helper must follow its own data-handling and report requirements; it is never a prerequisite. Do not expose private source material in shared documentation. Continue independent work while the affected explanation waits. Small corrections reuse established context rather than restarting discovery.

Keep a short baseline in the existing authoritative project design or associated maintenance page: reader/outcome, supported value and sources, required content and purpose, reading paths, medium/language/viewport, decisions and open checks. Do not create a competing registry.

## Choose and draft the reader paths

When both first understanding and repeat use matter, provide directly accessible, consistent paths sharing setup and technical facts:

- **Quickstart:** brief bullet answers to purpose, specific value, fit, prerequisites, first action and expected result. No story before the action.
- **Presentation:** connected introduction, explanation and useful ending. Explain purpose and relevant difference before examples; lead directly to the quickstart.

A focused how-to or lookup can use one path. Presentation need not mean slides. Assess entries as a newcomer with sound judgment and little subject knowledge, without condescension.

Introductory entries require four things together: a useful small graphic, plain language, benefit/graphic/next step in one readable first view, and clear reader direction. A pure lookup may omit a graphic or narrative that adds no orientation; this exception does not waive the introductory requirements.

Check overload, condescension/boredom and direction before expanding. For adoption guides, remove the example temporarily and check that the specific value still stands. Repair weak entries first. For a substantial redesign, show concrete entry drafts and obtain feedback; an already approved direction or a small requested edit needs no new approval round.

Use the actual destination viewport, provisionally 1366 × 768 at 100% zoom for browser guides. Include surrounding wiki or application navigation. Check mobile when required; never shrink text or hide essentials to force a fit. A source or desktop check is not evidence of fit in another viewer.

## Develop or revise

Connect explanation/action to expected result. Introduce prerequisites and material warnings before use, explain necessary terms, provide recovery for common failures and end by fulfilling the opening promise. Keep deeper sections understandable. Use short connected paragraphs, lists for steps and tables for comparisons. Remove filler.

For revisions, identify affected decisions before editing. Preserve their **meaning, prominence and location in the needed reader path**, not just keywords. A shortened introductory quickstart retains its brief purpose/value answer in that quickstart. Unchanged requirements still apply. Afterwards record each affected decision's disposition, destination and actual check in the existing baseline; check for accidental loss elsewhere. Resolve genuine conflicts before dropping requirements.

Use destination-supported graphics with readable labels and text equivalents; color alone cannot carry meaning. Preserve promised offline use. Moving between HTML, Markdown and wikis preserves purpose and navigation, not a fixed template. Unsupported diagram syntax is not a rendered graphic.

## Check and deliver

Review the affected reader journey, setup, links, examples, shared facts and success signs. Inspect the actual destination and follow actionable instructions where possible. For a substantial guide, an available native reviewer may check supplied decisions and return omissions with locations and concrete repairs. Keep that optional review read-only and bounded, without further delegation. Small edits need no helper.

Update the baseline and hand over the artifact, actual checks and open points. Check destination access before publishing; an inaccessible destination gets a transferable draft with the remaining check stated. Publication requires authorization. Local structure checks do not prove readability, publication or newcomer success. Use enough checking for the changed work; no mandatory phase system, scoring program or review loop.
