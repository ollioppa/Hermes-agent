# Economic Mechanism Audit

## Trigger and purpose

Use before accepting or rejecting company, household, platform, labor, property or credit examples as cross-sector evidence. Lead with the mechanism in ordinary language; precision follows only when it changes direction, phase, bearer or transmission.

## Audit procedure

1. **Freeze claim and phase.** Separate existence, prevalence, magnitude, transmission and market timing. A non-representative example can establish existence, not macro scale. Locate build/subsidize → scale → harvest → defend → compress → restructure; inspect the profit source, not profit alone.
2. **Choose the structural anchor.** Define the regime transition before selecting comparison years. A pre-pandemic/pre-crisis endpoint is not automatically healthy. Split households by income, wealth, tenure, age and financing vintage; firms by size, market power and state support. Separate self-funded private demand from fiscal replacement. Aggregate stability can conceal a deteriorating median.
3. **Rebuild the economic unit.** Trace product, distribution, financing, service/aftersales, resale, subscriptions and advertising. Identify capital and costs carried outside the focal firm by workers, suppliers, dealers, hosts, customers or the state. Company ROIC is not system ROIC.
4. **Set the clocks.** Compare technical usefulness, rent life, capital commitments and household payment duration; include inventory, refinancing, replacement and technology cadence. Obligations outlasting use are not inherently harmful: test residual value, continuing benefit, income, interest, exit rights and risk allocation. Never impose one lag across sectors.
5. **Test demand architecture and customer value.** Follow discovery → choice/checkout → use/renewal → exit. Inspect pricing, incentives, financing and friction without inferring manipulation from their existence. Separate actual use and voluntary repeat behavior from payments sustained by lock-in; test churn after minimum terms. Trace customer surplus, trust and repairability alongside revenue and margin.
6. **Separate incremental demand from displacement.** Decompose revenue into real units/use, realized net price and mix, using the business model's actual identity rather than multiplying percentage changes. Promotions can create additional demand, pull purchases forward or divert competitors' demand. Inspect post-promotion behavior, support cost per real unit, retention and contribution margin; higher ad spend alone proves neither weakness nor profitable growth.
7. **Recombine obligations without double counting.** Trace fees, cards, BNPL, leases, subscriptions and automatic payments against the same pay cycle; net gig income of operating costs. Keep consumption, payments due this period and future commitments separate. A financed purchase is not fresh consumption again at principal repayment; interest/fees are additional financing costs. Do not subtract a contract payment already included in essential spending. Missing consolidated credit reporting constrains measurement, not evidence of harmlessness; distinguish bank-style supervision from the overbroad label `unregulated`.
8. **Test learning and adaptation symmetrically.** Adjacent-sector experience with reviews, creators, platform trust, low-loss trials and switching can accelerate adoption. Distinguish viral trial/status change from repeat behavior and durable economics. Cash alone is not transformation capacity: test adjacent knowledge, simplicity, self-cannibalization and demonstrated capability conversion.
9. **Require a genuine counterpath.** Match shock, phase and economic unit. Seek sustained viable volume/use and cash conversion without rising support intensity, hidden externalization or fresh capital binding. Profits from harvest or rent migration are not proof of healthy adaptation. If none is found, state that boundary rather than inventing balancing benefits.
10. **Close the bridge.** Trace loss → holder/financier → response → employment, investment, consumption, collateral or another borrower. Mark arrows `proven`, `plausible` or `absent`. Distinguish rent migration from aggregate loss; connected symptoms are not independent shocks. Broad recovery requires the payer-to-beneficiary chain to improve disposable cashflow, job quality and private activity. Preserve original forecasts; new mechanisms do not backdate evidence or bypass revision rules.

## Evidence discipline

- Read the original report in full. For Google Docs use plain-text export for complete content and rendered/HTML pages when links need recovery.
- Before alleging a gap, inventory mechanism, analogy, phase/lag, buffers, rent migration, counterpath, trigger and falsifier against the report's sections. Retract objections already answered; sound work does not require manufactured criticism.
- Prefer original filings, regulators, official statistics and company disclosures. Record period, population, comparison basis, counterevidence and alternative causes; distinguish observed data, inference and forecast.
- Verify each source supports the exact claim: prevalence studies do not establish experimental efficacy; experiments do not supply a macro spending share. Missing public data is an explicit boundary, not a fabricated proxy.

## Output and ownership

Give one directional conclusion, then mechanism → phase → full-system cost bearer → sector clock → genuine counterexample → unresolved transmission link. Use only the detail that carries the judgment; do not create a score, mandatory dashboard or extra investment gate.
