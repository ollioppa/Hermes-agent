# Documentation Guide

**Readable for humans.** Apply this method in the already assigned writing step. The private adaptation history is not a runtime dependency.

Use the tools and writing step already assigned to the task. In projects with an editorial module, apply these instructions there; do not add a second editor, extra model call, reviewer fleet or post-processing layer. This skill grants no permission to send, publish, change project mandates or alter recurring jobs.

## Establish the assignment

Read the request, authoritative subject sources/design, current text and relevant prior decisions. Establish the concrete question, starting state, outcome, medium and available evidence before choosing examples. Reuse known answers. For direct communication the reader is the owner: an experienced IT professional with sound judgment, not a financial analyst. Explain unfamiliar subject knowledge without explaining obvious computing concepts or talking down to him. If he explicitly requests material for someone else, use that named audience.

Extract the subject's purpose, mechanisms, alternatives and material limits. Distinguish intended design, implemented behavior, observed results, interpretation and forecast. Do not invent benefits, uniqueness, superiority or measured savings. A good example cannot redefine the subject's primary scope.

For an adoption guide, explain the specific contribution compared with the familiar alternative, including when that alternative is sufficient. The reason must still stand if the example is removed. For a how-to, establish the task and result; do not force an adoption pitch. For a briefing, establish the relevant subject question and the source-supported judgment, not a story about the reporting machinery.

Resolve material remaining gaps through the task's existing discovery/research owner and available tools, or ask a focused question if needed. Respect sourcebook-only and other bounded editorial mandates: do not initiate new research or rewrite a specialist judgment during a wording-only pass. Continue independent work while the affected claim waits. Small corrections reuse established context rather than restarting discovery.

For maintained documents, keep a short baseline in the existing project design or associated maintenance page: reader/outcome, supported purpose/value and sources, required content, reading paths, medium and decisions/open checks. Do not create a competing source of truth or a per-message registry for chat and routine mail.

## Choose and draft the reader paths

When first understanding and repeated action/lookup are both needed, provide directly accessible paths sharing setup, sources and technical facts:

- **Quickstart:** brief bullet answers to purpose, specific value, fit, prerequisites, first action and expected result. Do not place a story before the action. A shortened introductory quickstart retains its own brief purpose/value answer; its survival elsewhere is not enough.
- **Presentation:** connected introduction, explanation and useful ending. Explain purpose and relevant difference before examples, and lead directly to action or the quickstart when appropriate. Presentation does not mean slides.

A focused explanation, how-to, lookup or message can use one path. Do not produce two complete versions of every mail. Offer deeper detail without making it a prerequisite for understanding the main answer; do not replace explicitly requested full material with a summary.

Assess introductory guide and start-page entries for all four together: **plain language, a small useful graphic, benefit/graphic/next step in one readable first view, and clear reader direction**. Check overload, condescension/boredom and missing direction before expanding the document. For a substantial redesign without an already approved direction, show concrete entry drafts and obtain feedback before expanding the whole document. Use feedback already given; small edits and an approved direction need no redundant approval round.

Use the actual destination viewport. For browser guides, 1366 × 768 at 100% zoom is provisional until specified; include surrounding UI and check narrow/mobile views when required. Never shrink text or hide essentials to claim a fit. Do not transfer that desktop pixel condition to every Telegram message or market mail. A pure lookup may omit narrative and graphics that add no orientation; this does not remove the introductory-guide requirements.

## Develop or revise

Lead with the concrete meaning, then use the numbers as evidence. the owner should not have to infer the economic or practical consequence from isolated metrics. Introduce necessary knowledge where it becomes useful. When explaining a specialist term in everyday language, include the corresponding technical term in parentheses at the first explanation so he can recognize it elsewhere. Keep the canonical term accurate; do not manufacture jargon merely to add a parenthesis.

When judgment is requested, state a reasoned position and develop its strongest support: what is observed, how the cause leads to the consequence, and why it matters to the question. Weight arguments by their bearing on the conclusion, not their count or paragraph length. Several descriptions of one mechanism are not independent evidence. Give consequential arguments enough depth; keep supporting context proportionate.

**Facts must be correct. Forecasts may be wrong.** Within the assigned analytical mandate, give the expected future when a forecast is requested and explain the decisive support and what would change the judgment. Keep observed facts and assumptions distinct. Do not retreat into an equal list of possibilities merely because the future is uncertain. In a wording-only revision, preserve the existing forecast and judgment; a stronger voice is not permission for a stronger investment call.

Test the important objection and give it its actual weight. If it changes the judgment, change the judgment within the authorized analytical task, or flag the conflict to its owner during editing. Otherwise do not reflexively cancel each clear sentence with a generic counter-possibility. Preserve material counterevidence and limits where they affect the argument. Do not perform conviction unsupported by the sources.

Connect explanation/action to expected result. A practical instruction says where to act, what to enter/select and how to recognize success. Put prerequisites and material warnings before the action, and provide a concrete recovery for common failures. Keep deeper sections understandable too.

Use short connected paragraphs and explanatory headings; lists for actual steps and compact comparisons where useful. Every paragraph advances the reader's question. Remove repeated claims, inflated abstractions, obvious explanations and commentary about your own writing. There is no universal word limit, compulsory pro/con quota, opinion in every status update or mandatory closing question. A confirmation can be one sentence.

Before revising, identify the affected prior decisions. Preserve their **meaning, prominence and location in the needed reading path**, not only their keywords: reasoning, quantities, units, dates, sample bases, assumptions, source attribution, forecast horizon and significant counterevidence. Do not turn a plan into a completed action or an old measurement into today's event. Do not strengthen uncertain provenance during a rewrite. Resolve genuine conflicts before silently dropping a requirement and check unaffected parts for accidental losses. For each affected decision in maintained documents, record its disposition (preserved, rephrased, moved or deliberately replaced), current location and actual check result in the existing baseline. Routine messages do not need this record.

## Fit the medium and give visual orientation

- **Mail:** retain **Introduction → Body → Summary**, with a specific subject. The opening establishes topic, relevance and the central answer; the body develops the connected explanation and weighted evidence; the summary closes the opening question without new arguments, a full repetition or a generic retreat. Literal section titles are optional. Keep operations logs out of the opening. Preserve requested full source material in the body or an unchanged attachment as appropriate.
- **Telegram:** continue the conversation with readable paragraphs and supported Markdown. Avoid wide tables and unnecessary repetition of full reports in follow-ups. Do not force a mail template or graphic onto an acknowledgment.
- **Guides, HTML and wikis:** retain directly accessible paths when both are needed, shared facts and useful graphics. Use supported destination elements and preserve promised offline use; no compulsory renderer or publishing system.
- **Substantial briefings and weeklies:** preserve useful charts and visual summaries; choose a compact graphic showing the main development, comparison or causal relationship when the evidence and medium support it. Graphics are part of understanding, not decoration. Do not silently remove an existing agreed chart or required historical series while simplifying prose. Use existing supported charts, bars, text plots or relationship diagrams, with readable labels and a text equivalent. Never invent missing observations or interpolate an unobserved series just to complete a graphic.

Color alone cannot carry essential meaning. Raw diagram syntax is not a rendered graphic if the destination cannot display it. Use interactivity only where supported reliably; verify before relying on it for essential content. A local browser preview is not proof of behavior in an email client or on a phone. Routine messages need no illustrative image or image-generation call.

Keep strict machine-readable outputs and requested original traces intact; do not add explanatory prose or graphics to a fixed JSON/text contract. Reader prose may omit operational internals, but a requested original artifact remains unchanged.

## Check and deliver

Reread the affected reader journey: does the opening orient the owner, does the body explain the important connections, and does the ending fulfill the opening promise? Check sources, examples, shared facts, links, prerequisites, success signs and preservation of earlier decisions. Ask whether the reader still has to guess what a number or label means. Fix the text in the existing writing step, not by constructing a new workflow.

Inspect the actual destination and exercise actionable instructions where possible. Scale checking to the change; there is no mandatory separate reviewer or model round per message. Report only checks actually performed. Local structure checks do not prove comprehension, publication, email delivery or recipient-host behavior.

For maintained material update its existing baseline, and hand over the requested artifact with specific remaining checks. Check access before publishing; an inaccessible destination gets a transferable draft, not a claim of publication. Delivery requires the existing authorized mail/publishing workflow and verification.

Existing operator instructions, specialist analysis, product/output contracts and delivery permissions remain binding. Use the existing editorial module or `docs/research/editorial/SKILL.md` for its assigned work, and the established mail skills when sending. Installation of this skill alone does not reconfigure project prompts or scheduled jobs.
