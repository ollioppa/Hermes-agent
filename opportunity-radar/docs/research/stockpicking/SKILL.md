## Common decision and evidence invariants

1. Explain the business and shareholder claim before specialist metrics. Separate a good company, an attractive price and a useful portfolio role. Macro/sector analysis is context and transmission, not an independent alpha selector.
2. Use Business → Debate → Evidence → Price → Decision. Name at most three claims carrying the judgment, with direct counterevidence and consequences if false; keep a compact audit trace for other material areas. Broad investigation does not require a long reader checklist.
3. Separate technological success from shareholder rent capture; compare rent life with capital payback and priced duration. Test attack and renewal symmetrically. Investigate positive asymmetry as rigorously as risk. Do not count one mechanism repeatedly as different defects.
4. Primary evidence first; publication timestamp owns point-in-time eligibility. Search material developments after the last periodic release. Label reported, normalized and forward figures; bind price, instrument and timestamp. Missing disclosures are limits, not invented strengths or automatic research dispositions.
5. Apply the frozen investment policy once at the decision boundary. Do not tighten or relax admission from a new narrative. Low price cannot repair undefined cash ownership; multiple independent repairs are not one WATCH condition. Customer-treatment exclusions and portfolio overlap remain personal decision reasons, not invented company damage.
6. Keep screening, comparative routing, completed research, investment admission and executed trades distinct. `DEEP_DIVE` is first-pass routing only. A completed positive case is a decision-ready proposal to the owner, not an automatic status write. Research-revisit triggers are not WATCH promotion conditions; never invent a condition for a direct-ready case. Archival ends an opportunity, not permanently excludes the security; a later admitted opportunity gets a new case while old IDs, frozen baselines and holdings links remain intact.
7. Research automation may make only its contract-authorized Candidate decision; only the owner decides WATCH/READY admission, permanent exclusion and trades. No mail/report implies state mutation. Verify any separately authorized write through its real consumer and target.
